    Coverage results¶

    The functional coverage of the project's regression, merged over every test and seed: which bins were hit, how often and by which tests, and which bins no run hit (the holes). Every run writes its covergroup bins to a coverage database; the report merges them. How the covergroups are built and where they live: Functional coverage. The bins of each coverage collector are also in its panel on the project map.

    Merged over 100 runs: 222 of 222 bins hit. A percentage is covered bins / bins of the covergroup, as get_inst_coverage() gives it in the log of one run.
